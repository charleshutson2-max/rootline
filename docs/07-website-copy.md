## 4. Membership — `/membership`

**Title:** Membership

**Lead:** Two paths through the front door.

**Path A — Invite code**  
If a Member or Steward sent you a code, enter it after you create an account. Steward-issued codes may complete faster; Member-issued codes are confirmed by a Steward.

**Path B — Relationship claim**  
Share your full name, how you are related, and optionally a voucher relative and supporting documents. A Steward will Approve, Request changes, or Decline — always with a reason.

**Minors:** A parent or guardian must approve the account and the child’s visibility.

**Until you are approved:** You will see this public site, an optional deceased Honor Roll if enabled, and this request form — not living-family records.

**Form labels (draft):** Full name · Asserted relationship · Optional voucher · Optional documents · Submit claim  

**Secondary:** Already have a code? Enter invite code

## 7. Privacy — `/privacy`

**Title:** Privacy

**Lead:** Rootline is a private family archive with a thin public front.

**Principles (draft):**
- Living-family records require Steward-approved membership.  
- Living adults control address, social links, and new photos about themselves.  
- Stewards cannot publish those living fields without the person’s (or a parent’s, if minor) approval.  
- Ask Rootline answers from approved records only; it does not invent ancestors, dates, or ranks; it does not reveal living private contact details.  
- Public pages do not show living private photos or addresses.  
- Public History Mode for deceased generations is a future option and remains off unless Stewards enable it later.  
- Exports and succession exist so the archive can outlive any single Steward.  
- Media is delivered with short-lived signed links inside the app, not as a public phone book.

**Contact:** Privacy questions → Stewards / contact.
